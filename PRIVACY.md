# EpisodeFlow Privacy

Last updated: 2026-07-12

EpisodeFlow does not create an account, display advertising, or send analytics to the developer.

The app reads video metadata and thumbnails from media that the user permits Android to share. Playlists, playback positions, watched status, intro-skip settings, audio-track preferences, and playlist source links are stored locally in the app's private storage.

EpisodeFlow includes an optional embedded browser for source links selected by the user. Websites opened in that browser may apply their own privacy, cookie, login, and download policies. EpisodeFlow stores the latest visited source URL locally for the selected playlist but does not collect or transmit website login credentials to the developer.

Removing EpisodeFlow clears its private local settings through Android's normal uninstall process. Downloaded video files remain on the device unless the user removes them separately.
